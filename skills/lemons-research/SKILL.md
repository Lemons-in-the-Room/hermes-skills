# Lemons Research Skill

High-rigor web and market research for healthcare, clinical VR, and medical device regulations.

## When to Use

- Competitor intelligence (HypnoVR, OnComfort, XRHealth, SyncVR, Senopi).
- Regulatory checks: EU MDR Class I (SRN `IT-MF-000053057`), DiGA (Germany), PECAN (France), AIFA (Italy).
- Hospital tenders, grants, and market sizing checks.
- Fact-checking claims before including them in official decks, proposals, or board reports.

## Core Rules

1. **Never answer from memory** on volatile data: pricing, regulations, market stats, competitor updates.
2. **Multi-source verification**: Minimum 2-3 independent sources for any strategic claim.
3. **Flag contradictions**: If sources disagree, present both ranges instead of guessing.
4. **Always cite sources**: Provide entity name and direct URL.

## Industry Angles

- **Regulatory**: Prioritize official portals (EUR-Lex, BfArM, Ministero della Salute, Notified Bodies) over blogs.
- **Competitors**: Differentiate clinical positioning vs wellness/entertainment VR.
- **Market Sizing**: Prefer bottom-up calculations (target departments × headsets × annual subscription) over generic top-down market reports.
