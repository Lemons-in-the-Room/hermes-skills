# Communication

## Channels
- Notion: single source of truth for decisions, documentation and context.
- Jira: tech tasks and epics.
- Google Chat: quick coordination, not documentation.

Rules:
- Conversations are short, decisions are written.
- Important decisions are always documented, never only in chat.
- If you block someone, say it immediately. Do not wait for standup.
- If something is unclear, improve the system, not the workaround.
- Every activity has an owner and a date.
- Passwords and credentials are never written in docs or chat.

## Rhythm
- Short daily standup, two-week sprints, quarterly objectives with retrospectives.
- Every commitment has an owner, a deadline and a follow-up.
- Every experiment: owner, deadline, execution, measurement, decision.

## Internal tone
Direct and informal between teammates. No stupid questions, especially in the first 30 days. Ask first, research later.

## Writing style (applies to anything written for Lemons)
- Short paragraphs, active voice, remove redundancy.
- Clarity over decoration. No filler, no cliches, no decorative emojis.
- No long dash characters.
- Lead with the point. Then actions, owners, deadlines.
- Structure with headings and short sections when the text is long.
- Emails: short, high impact, response-oriented, one clear ask.
- Strategy: analytical, concrete, with action steps. Ask: why does this matter strategically? Does it create leverage?
- Tickets and task descriptions: terse and concrete. Outcomes written as actions ("Place it in the Unity scene"). No padding, no hedging, no meta notes.

## Tone per audience
| Audience | Tone |
|---|---|
| Investor | Structured, metrics-driven |
| Hospital, clinician | Clinical, practical, evidence first |
| Press | Institutional, journalistic |
| Internal | Operational, precise |
| LinkedIn | Institutional and evidence-led: results, partnerships, founders' and clinicians' voices |
| Instagram | Emotional and visual: real moments from the wards, the team, events. Empathetic |

Brand: playful on the surface, scientific underneath. Use the new branding and the team's Canva templates (the old orange and navy look is retired).

## Language
Team and docs work in English and Italian. Match the language of the person or document you are replying to. Italian for Italian hospitals and press.

## Pre-send checklist
1. Is every number on the approved list?
2. Any therapeutic promise or unvalidated clinical claim? Remove.
3. Regulatory wording only as agreed?
4. Facts, assumptions, opinions clearly separated?
5. Is the ask or next action in the first lines?
6. Can any sentence be cut?
