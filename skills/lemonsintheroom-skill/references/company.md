# Company

## Identity
Lemons in the Room is a health-tech company building clinical virtual reality solutions for hospitals. We work at the intersection of healthcare, neuroscience, design and technology. Focus: measurable human impact inside real clinical environments.

Mission: To improve patients' quality of life by creating the best Digital Therapeutics in the universe.
Vision: To become the gold standard in Digital Therapeutics.

## Products
| Line | For | Use |
|---|---|---|
| LEMO | Adults and adolescents | Oncology, dialysis, pain management, procedures |
| LEMO Park | Pediatrics, ages 4 to 17 | Mascots and worlds built for children in hospital |
| LEMO Smile | Dental | Reducing anxiety in the dentist's chair |

LEMO is designed to:
- be easy for clinical staff to use
- work for people who cannot use smartphones or PCs
- integrate into hospital routines
- deliver consistent, repeatable experiences, specific to the medical procedure or treatment
- support both patients and healthcare professionals

Model: VR-as-a-Service subscription (headsets, content, training, support in one plug-and-play package).

## What we do NOT build
Entertainment VR, consumer wellness apps, generic mindfulness products.

## Audience
Buyers: university hospitals, IRCCS, oncology centres, pediatric hospitals, private clinics. Rule of two contacts per organisation: one clinical decision maker, one operational gatekeeper (procurement).

- Clinical (Head of Department, Nursing Coordinator, psychologists): want evidence, protocols, impact on the patient pathway.
- Management (Healthcare Director, Quality, Patient Experience, Innovation): want humanisation of care, quality indicators, budget fit.
- Ecosystem (patient associations, societies, KOLs, foundations, journalists): want real stories, credibility, shared mission.

## Direction
- Focus markets: Italy and EU hospitals.
- Framing for strategy and sales: adoption, clinical validation, institutional credibility.
