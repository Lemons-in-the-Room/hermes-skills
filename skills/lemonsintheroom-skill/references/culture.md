# Culture

## Values
| Value | Expected behavior | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Patients First | If it does not improve the patient experience, we do not do it. | Confusing "cool" with "useful". |
| Autonomy | Find your own solutions to work problems and do it. Express ideas at any time. | Always waiting for instructions. |
| Think Big | Set ambitious goals. Challenge existing standards. Look for big opportunities. | Settling for "good enough". Choosing goals because they are easy. |
| Scientific Approach | Follow the scientific method. | Presenting hypotheses or opinions as facts. |
| Speed of Execution | Do today what you can do tomorrow. Move with speed and vigor. | Getting comfortable. |
| No Excuses | No excuses, no softness. Do not go easy on yourself. Own the problem and bring a fix. | Blaming circumstances or others. Going easy on yourself. |

Motto: The impossible route is often the one that changes history. Non est ad astra mollis e terris via.

## Working agreements
- We challenge ideas, not people.
- Difficult topics are raised in the meeting, not afterward and not through third parties.
- Disagree openly. Once a decision is made, everyone commits to it (disagree and commit).
- Distinguish facts, assumptions and opinions.
- Every important decision has one owner. Consultation can be collective, accountability is individual.
- Every commitment has an owner, a deadline and a follow-up.
- Share bad news and problems promptly, neither late nor hidden.
- A new priority requires deciding what gets reduced, postponed or dropped.
- Do not reopen a decision without new facts.
- From mistakes, seek learning and process improvement, not a culprit.
- Do not leave a meeting without knowing what was decided.
- Whoever raises a problem brings at least one proposal.

## Team principles (Tech Playbook)
1. Knowledge is there to be shared. No gatekeeping. No question is too basic.
2. Ask first, research later. A quick question in chat saves hours.
3. To fail is to succeed. Experiment, document the learning, move on together.
4. Cooking is everything. The end result is the validation. Use AI as leverage, look for best recipes, do not reinvent the wheel, cook with your authentic style.

## How the team works
- Small team in complex environments. Clarity and autonomy matter more than hierarchy.
- Remote-first, async-friendly, trust-based.
- Multidisciplinary: psychology, medicine, design, technology.
- Strong documentation culture. Read, navigate, comment. Do not only receive tasks.
- Direct connection to patient impact is the main motivator.
- Raise problems early, with a proposal.
